# Luna Learns to Share - Launch Marketing Plan

Complete marketing strategy to get your first sales and reviews.

---

## Pre-Launch (1 Week Before Publication)

### Day 1-2: Set Up Author Platform

**Create Author Website (2-3 hours):**
1. Register domain: [yourname]books.com or [yourname]author.com
   - Use Namecheap or Google Domains: ~$12/year

2. Create simple website using Carrd.co (free) or WordPress
   - Homepage: Author bio, photo, book cover
   - Books page: Luna Learns to Share with description
   - Contact page: Email form
   - **Email signup:** Offer free short book for subscribers

3. Set up email marketing:
   - MailerLite (free up to 1,000 subscribers)
   - Create welcome email sequence
   - Connect to website signup form

**Create Social Media Profiles:**
- Instagram: @[yourname]books
  - Bio: "Children's book author 📚 Teaching kids about friendship & kindness ✨ New book: Luna Learns to Share 🐰"
  - Profile pic: Your photo or Luna character
  - Link: Your website

- Pinterest: [Your Name] Books
  - Create boards: "Books About Sharing," "Children's Books I Love," "Parenting Tips"

- Facebook: Author page (optional)

### Day 3-4: Create Marketing Content

**Social Media Graphics (Canva):**
1. Book announcement graphic
   - Cover image + "Coming Soon!" text
   - Include publication date

2. Character introduction posts (4 posts):
   - Luna: "Meet Luna! She's learning that sharing makes everything more fun!"
   - Ollie: "This is Ollie the otter. He loves playing with friends!"
   - Maya: "Maya the mouse is shy but kind."
   - Benny: "Benny the bear loves to color and share!"

3. Book quotes graphics (5-7):
   - Pull touching quotes from the book
   - Design pretty quote graphics

4. Behind-the-scenes:
   - Show your process (character design evolution)
   - "How I created this book" story

**Email Content:**
- Welcome email (auto-send when someone subscribes)
- Launch announcement email
- Request for reviews email

**Pinterest Pins:**
- Book cover with link to Amazon (once live)
- "Best Books About Sharing for Preschoolers" pin
- "Teaching Kids to Share: Book Recommendations"

### Day 5-7: Build Review Team

**Compile List of Book Reviewers:**

**Parent/Teacher Influencers on Instagram:**
1. Search hashtags: #teachersofinstagram #parentblogger #bookstagram #kidlit
2. Find accounts with 5K-50K followers (micro-influencers more responsive)
3. Make list of 20-30 accounts
4. Note their email or DM contact

**Book Review Blogs:**
- Google "children's book review blog"
- Look for blogs that accept self-published books
- Make list of 15-20 blogs with contact emails

**BookTok/BookTube:**
- Search YouTube/TikTok for children's book reviewers
- Add 10-15 creators to list

**Teacher/Parent Groups on Facebook:**
- Join 5-10 relevant groups:
  - Preschool teachers
  - Kindergarten teachers
  - Parents of toddlers/preschoolers
  - Homeschool parents
- Read rules! (Most don't allow direct promotion)

**Create Review Request Email Template:**
```
Subject: Children's Book Review Opportunity - Luna Learns to Share

Hi [Name],

I'm [Your Name], a children's book author, and I recently came across your [blog/Instagram] and loved your review of [mention a book they reviewed].

I'm launching my new picture book, "Luna Learns to Share," which teaches preschoolers about friendship, kindness, and the joy of sharing through the story of Luna the bunny and her friends.

The book is perfect for children ages 3-6 and is being published on [DATE] via Amazon KDP.

I'd love to send you a complimentary review copy (PDF or print - your choice). If you're interested and have space in your review schedule, please let me know!

You can see the cover and learn more here: [your website link]

Thank you for considering, and keep up the wonderful work supporting children's literature!

Best,
[Your Name]
[Your Email]
[Your Website]
```

## Launch Week (Days 1-7 After Going Live)

### DAY 1: Launch Day! 🎉

**Morning:**
- [ ] Verify book is LIVE on Amazon
- [ ] Check listing (no errors, "Look Inside" working)
- [ ] Copy Amazon links (paperback and Kindle)
- [ ] Take screenshots of listing

**Afternoon - Social Media Blitz:**

**Instagram:**
- [ ] Post #1: Cover reveal + announcement
  - Caption: "I'm thrilled to announce my new book is LIVE! 🎉📚 Luna Learns to Share is now available on Amazon..."
  - Include link in bio
  - Hashtags: #childrensbooks #kidslit #newbook #picturebook #sharingiscaring #kidsbooks #parenting #preschool #teachersofinstagram

- [ ] Story series (10+ stories):
  - "It's launch day!"
  - Book cover
  - Swipe-through of 3-4 pages
  - Character introductions
  - Link to Amazon
  - "Thank you for your support!"

**Pinterest:**
- [ ] Pin cover image with Amazon link (5 different boards)
- [ ] Pin "Books About Sharing" list graphic

**Facebook:**
- [ ] Post on your page
- [ ] Share in 1-2 groups (only if rules allow, provide value first)

**Email:**
- [ ] Send to friends and family:
  - "I've published my first children's book!"
  - Tell the story behind why you wrote it
  - Amazon link
  - "If you purchase, an honest review would mean the world to me"

### DAY 2: Outreach Begins

**Email Book Reviewers (10-15 emails):**
- [ ] Send personalized emails to bloggers from your list
- [ ] Offer free PDF or print copy
- [ ] No pressure, friendly tone

**Social Media:**
- [ ] Instagram: Behind-the-scenes post
  - "How I created Luna Learns to Share"
  - Show character evolution
- [ ] Story: Q&A about the book
- [ ] Pinterest: Create more pins

### DAY 3: More Outreach + Engagement

**Influencer Outreach (10-15 DMs):**
- [ ] DM parent/teacher influencers on Instagram
  - "Hi [Name], I love your content about [topic]. I just published a children's book about sharing and would love to send you a free copy if you're interested in checking it out!"

**Engagement:**
- [ ] Comment on posts in relevant hashtags (#childrensbooks, #teachersofinstagram)
- [ ] Respond to any comments on your posts
- [ ] Join conversations in Facebook groups (don't promote yet)

**Content:**
- [ ] Instagram post: Character spotlight (Luna)
- [ ] Share a lesson from the book: "3 Ways to Teach Your Child About Sharing"

### DAY 4-5: Run Promotion (Optional)

**If enrolled in KDP Select, consider:**
- [ ] Make Kindle version FREE for 3 days
- [ ] Or run Countdown Deal ($0.99)

**Announce promotion:**
- [ ] Instagram: "For 3 days only, get Luna Learns to Share FREE on Kindle!"
- [ ] Email: "Free Book Alert!"
- [ ] Pinterest: Update pins with "FREE for limited time"
- [ ] Post in freebie groups/forums

**Why this works:**
- Drives downloads
- Increases visibility on Amazon
- Generates reviews faster
- Builds email list (offer related free content)

### DAY 6-7: Continue Momentum

**Content Creation:**
- [ ] Instagram: Share a touching quote from the book
- [ ] Create "reader response" post if anyone has purchased
- [ ] Instagram Reel: Flip through the book (15-30 seconds)
- [ ] Post on TikTok too (same video)

**More Outreach:**
- [ ] Email another 10 reviewers
- [ ] Reach out to 10 more influencers
- [ ] Submit to NetGalley or BookSirens (optional, $0-50)

- Character content: Fun facts about Luna and friends

**Outreach (Daily):**

- DM 5 new influencers

**Engagement:**
- Respond to all comments and DMs
- Thank anyone who posts about your book
- Join conversations in parent/teacher communities

## Week 3-4: Sustain & Scale

### Continue Regular Posting

**Instagram Strategy:**
- 3-5 feed posts per week
- Daily stories
- Engage with followers
- Use consistent hashtags
- Tag relevant accounts when appropriate

**Email Marketing:**
- Send update to list: Sales milestone, thank you, request reviews
- Share educational content related to book theme
- Build relationship with subscribers

### Monitor & Adjust

**Check KDP Dashboard Weekly:**
- [ ] Sales numbers (print + Kindle + KU pages)
- [ ] Reviews count
- [ ] Amazon Best Sellers Rank in your categories

**Optimize Listing if Needed:**
- [ ] Adjust keywords based on what's working
- [ ] Improve description if needed
- [ ] Update cover if it's not converting

### Proof Copy Review

**When proof arrives:**
- [ ] Check print quality
- [ ] Look for any issues
- [ ] Take photos for social media
- [ ] Share "Proof copy arrived!" post
- [ ] If issues: update files on KDP

## Budget for Launch Marketing

### Free Options (Budget: $0)
- Social media posting (Instagram, Pinterest, Facebook)
- Email to friends/family
- Blogger/influencer outreach
- Organic engagement in groups
- **Cost: $0**

### Minimal Budget ($50-100)
- KDP proof copy: $10
- Review service (NetGalley/BookSirens): $0-50
- Social media graphics (Canva Pro): $13
- **Cost: ~$23-73**

### Moderate Budget ($100-200)
- Above items: $73
- Facebook ads: $50-100 (test if you want)
- Additional review copies to mail: $20-50
- **Cost: ~$143-223**

**Recommendation:** Start with free/minimal budget for first book. Invest more in ads once you have 3-5 books.

## Remember:

✨ **First book is the hardest** - Each subsequent book gets easier and faster
