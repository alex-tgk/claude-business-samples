# Amazon KDP Publishing Workflow

Step-by-step guide to publishing your children's book on Amazon Kindle Direct Publishing (KDP).

---

## Prerequisites

Before starting, ensure you have:
- [ ] Completed book files (interior PDF, cover PDF, eBook files)
- [ ] Amazon KDP account set up with tax information
- [ ] Book description and keywords prepared
- [ ] Author bio ready
- [ ] High-quality cover image for thumbnail
- [ ] Decided on pricing strategy

---

## Part 1: Account Setup (One-Time, 30 minutes)

### Step 1: Create/Verify KDP Account

1. Go to [kdp.amazon.com](https://kdp.amazon.com)
2. Sign in with Amazon account or create new one
3. Complete account information:
   - Author/Publisher name
   - Email address
   - Phone number

### Step 2: Tax Information

1. Go to Account > Payment and Tax Information
2. Complete tax interview:
   - **US authors:** W-9 form
   - **International:** W-8BEN form
3. Verify information is accepted (no errors)

### Step 3: Payment Information

1. Set up direct deposit (fastest, recommended) or check
2. **US:** Provide bank routing and account number
3. **International:** Provide SWIFT/BIC and IBAN
4. Save and verify

## Part 2: Create New Book - Paperback (45-60 minutes)

### Step 1: Start New Paperback Book

1. Go to KDP Bookshelf
2. Click "+ Create" > "Paperback"
3. You'll see three sections: Paperback Details, Paperback Content, Paperback Rights & Pricing

### Step 2: Paperback Details

**Language**
- Select: English (or your language)

**Book Title**
- Enter full title exactly as it appears on cover
- **Tip:** Include keywords naturally (e.g., "Luna Learns to Be Brave: A Story About Overcoming Fear")

**Subtitle** (Optional but Recommended)
- Clarifies book content
- Great for SEO keywords
- Example: "A Children's Book About Courage and Self-Confidence for Ages 4-8"

**Series** (If applicable)
- Series title: "Emotional Adventures" (or your series name)
- Volume number: 1, 2, 3, etc.
- **Note:** Series help with discoverability and reader loyalty

**Edition Number**
- Leave as "1" unless this is a revision

**Author**
- Add yourself as primary author
- Can add "Illustrated by" as contributor if listing illustration credits

**Contributors** (Optional)
- Illustrator, Editor, etc.
- If using AI, no need to list—you are the author/creator

**Description**
This is crucial for sales! Write 2-3 paragraphs:

**Paragraph 1:** Hook parents with the problem/theme
```
Is your child struggling with [problem]? [Title] is a heartwarming story that helps young children understand [theme] through the adventures of [character].
```

**Paragraph 2:** Story overview (no spoilers!)
```
Follow [character] as they [brief plot summary]. Through [character]'s journey, children will learn [lesson] in a fun, engaging way.
```

**Paragraph 3:** Features and benefits
```
Perfect for ages [X-X], this beautifully illustrated book features:
• [Benefit 1, e.g., "Diverse characters that reflect real families"]
• [Benefit 2, e.g., "Simple language perfect for bedtime reading"]
• [Benefit 3, e.g., "Conversation starters for parents and children"]
• [Benefit 4, e.g., "Stunning full-color illustrations on every page"]

Ideal for bedtime reading, classroom libraries, or as a thoughtful gift!
```

**Publishing Rights**
- Select: "I own the copyright and I hold the necessary publishing rights"
- You created it with AI tools = you own rights

**Keywords** (7 slots - use them ALL!)
Research keywords using:
- Amazon search suggestions
- Competitor books
- Keyword tools (Publisher Rocket, Kindlepreneur)

**Good keyword examples:**
- children's books about emotions
- anger management for kids
- feelings books for preschoolers
- social emotional learning books
- diverse children's books
- books about friendship for kids ages 4-8
- bedtime stories for toddlers

**Bad keywords:**
- book (too broad)
- children (too broad)
- emotions (too broad)
- bestseller (not descriptive)

**Categories** (Choose 2, but can request up to 10 via KDP support)
- Click "Select categories"
- Choose most specific categories possible
- **Example path:** Books > Children's Books > Growing Up & Facts of Life > Friendship, Social Skills & School Life > Emotions & Feelings

**Pro tip:** After publishing, email KDP support to add 8 more categories (BISAC codes)

**Age Range and Grade Level**
- Be specific and honest
- Example: "3 - 7 years" or "Preschool - 2nd Grade"
- Helps Amazon show book to right audience

**Pre-order** (Optional)
- Skip for first book
- Can use for later books to build buzz

**Save as Draft** before proceeding!

### Step 3: Paperback Content

**Print Options**
- **Interior type:** Color or Black & White
  - Children's books: almost always COLOR
  - **Note:** Color printing costs more but is essential for children's books

- **Paper type:**
  - White paper (standard, recommended)
  - Cream paper (for chapter books, not picture books)

- **Trim size:** Choose your book dimensions
  - **Most common for children's:** 8.5" x 8.5"
  - Other options: 8" x 10", 8.5" x 11"
  - Must match your PDF dimensions exactly

- **Bleed settings:**
  - Select "Bleed" if your images extend to page edges (most children's books)
  - Select "No Bleed" if you have white borders

- **Paperback cover finish:**
  - Glossy (recommended for children's books - more durable, colors pop)
  - Matte (sophisticated but shows wear faster)

**ISBN**
- **Recommended:** Use free Amazon ISBN
  - Free
  - Book listed under "Independently published"
  - Can only sell on Amazon
- **Alternative:** Buy your own ISBN ($125+ from Bowker)
  - Your own publisher name
  - Can sell anywhere
  - **Note:** Not necessary for beginners

**Upload Manuscript**
1. Click "Upload paperback manuscript"
2. Select your interior PDF file
3. Wait for upload and processing (1-5 minutes)
4. Review for any errors or warnings

**Common upload issues:**
- "Image resolution too low" → Re-export at 300 DPI minimum
- "Page count mismatch" → Verify PDF has correct number of pages
- "Bleed not detected" → Check your export settings include bleed

**Upload Cover**
1. Click "Upload a cover you already have"
2. Upload your cover PDF (created using KDP cover calculator)
3. Wait for processing

**Alternative:** Use KDP Cover Creator
- Only if you don't have design software
- Limited templates
- Not recommended for professional results

**Preview Your Book**
1. Click "Preview" button (appears after uploads complete)
2. Online Previewer opens - CHECK EVERY PAGE:
   - [ ] All pages in correct order
   - [ ] Text is readable
   - [ ] Images look good
   - [ ] Cover looks correct
   - [ ] No weird formatting
   - [ ] Bleed extends properly

3. Download print-ready PDF to verify locally

**Approve Preview**
- Once satisfied, proceed to pricing

**Save as Draft** before proceeding!

### Step 4: Paperback Rights & Pricing

**Territories**
- **Recommended:** "All territories" (worldwide rights)
- Unless you have specific reasons to restrict

**Primary Marketplace Pricing**

**US Marketplace (Amazon.com):**
- KDP provides minimum price based on printing cost
- **Formula:** Print cost + desired royalty = list price
- **Standard royalty:** 60% of (List price - print cost)

**Example calculation:**
- Printing cost: $3.50 (32-page color book, 8.5x8.5)
- Desired royalty per sale: $4.00
- List price: $3.50 + $4.00/.60 = $10.17
- Round to: $10.99 or $11.99

**Pricing sweet spots for children's books:**
- Board book style: $6.99 - $8.99
- Picture books: $8.99 - $12.99
- Early readers: $7.99 - $10.99

**Other Marketplaces:**
- Check boxes for: UK, Germany, France, Spain, Italy, Japan, Canada, Australia
- Use "Expand all marketplaces" to see pricing
- Can set custom pricing or let Amazon adjust based on exchange rates
- **Recommended:** Let Amazon auto-adjust for first book

**Amazon's Expanded Distribution** (Optional)
- Makes book available to bookstores, libraries, online retailers
- Lower royalty (40% instead of 60%)
- **Recommended:** Enable it—extra exposure, no downside

**Save and Publish**
- Review all information one final time
- Click "Publish Your Paperback Book"
- **Processing time:** 24-72 hours for approval

## Part 3: Create eBook - Kindle Version (30-45 minutes)

Many parents prefer eBooks for tablets. Always create Kindle version!

### Step 1: Start New Kindle eBook

From Bookshelf:
1. Find your paperback book
2. Click "+ Kindle eBook" (creates linked edition)
3. **OR** Create from scratch: "+ Create" > "Kindle eBook"

### Step 2: Kindle eBook Details

**Most fields same as paperback:**
- Title, subtitle, author, description, keywords, categories (keep identical)
- KDP will auto-fill if linked to paperback

**eBook-Specific Settings:**
- **Digital Rights Management (DRM):**
  - Choose "No" (recommended - allows family sharing)
  - Prevents copying but annoys legitimate buyers
- **Book publication date:**
  - Leave blank to publish immediately
  - Or set future date for pre-order

### Step 3: Kindle eBook Content

**Upload eBook Manuscript:**

**Option A: Kindle Kids' Book Creator (Recommended for Picture Books)**
1. Download free tool: Kindle Kids' Book Creator
2. Import your page images (JPGs)
3. Add text to each page using tool
4. Choose layout: Text Pop-Up or Fixed Layout
   - **Fixed Layout:** Recommended for children's books (maintains design)
   - **Text Pop-Up:** Text can be read separately (good for early readers)
5. Export and upload .kpf file to KDP

**Option B: Upload PDF**
- Upload same PDF as print interior
- KDP converts automatically
- **Risk:** Formatting may not be perfect
- Always preview carefully!

**Option C: Create in KDP (Basic)**
- Not recommended for illustrated books
- Loses layout control

**Upload Cover Image**
- Upload front cover only (JPG or PNG)
- **Requirements:**
  - Minimum: 1000 pixels on shortest side
  - Recommended: 2560 x 1600 pixels
  - File size: under 50MB
- This is your book thumbnail on Amazon!

**Preview eBook**
1. Use online previewer (appears after upload)
2. Test on different devices: Kindle, iPad, phone
3. Check:
   - [ ] Images load correctly
   - [ ] Text is readable
   - [ ] Colors look good
   - [ ] Layout maintains integrity

### Step 4: Kindle eBook Pricing

**KDP Select (Optional but Recommended for Beginners)**
- Enrolls book in Kindle Unlimited
- **Benefits:**
  - Higher royalty option (70% instead of 35%)
  - Pages read generate income
  - Promotional tools (free days, countdown deals)
- **Drawback:** 90-day exclusivity (can't sell on other platforms)
- **Recommendation:** Try it for first 90 days, evaluate results

**Royalty Options:**
- **70% royalty:**
  - Price must be $2.99 - $9.99
  - Book must be enrolled in all geographies
  - Includes delivery costs (usually $0.01-0.15)
  - **Recommended for most children's books**

- **35% royalty:**
  - Any price
  - Use for books under $2.99 or over $9.99

**Pricing Strategy:**
- **Sweet spot:** $4.99 - $6.99 for children's eBooks
- Can price lower than print for easy "impulse buy"
- Consider: $9.99 print, $4.99 Kindle

**Example Royalty Calculation:**
- List price: $4.99
- Royalty rate: 70%
- Delivery cost: $0.10 (est. for illustrated book)
- Royalty per sale: ($4.99 x 0.70) - $0.10 = $3.39

**Kindle Unlimited (if enrolled):**
- Paid per page read
- Rate varies: ~$0.004 - $0.005 per page
- 32-page book read completely = $0.13 - $0.16
- **Note:** KU pages can add up with volume!

**MatchBook** (Optional)
- Offer discounted Kindle version when someone buys paperback
- Encourages both purchases

**Save and Publish**
- Click "Publish Your Kindle eBook"
- Goes live in 24-48 hours

## Part 4: After Publishing (1-3 days)

### Step 1: Verify Live Status (24-72 hours)

1. Check KDP Bookshelf for "Live" status
2. Once live, click "View on Amazon" to see listing
3. Verify:
   - [ ] Cover looks good
   - [ ] Title and description correct
   - [ ] Preview ("Look Inside") works
   - [ ] Correct categories and keywords showing
   - [ ] Both print and Kindle editions linked

**If not linked automatically:**
- Email KDP support to link paperback and Kindle editions
- Provides: ASINs of both versions, book title

### Step 2: Order Proof Copy

**Critical for first book!**
1. From Bookshelf, click "Order Proof Copy"
2. Pay production cost + shipping (~$10-15 total)
3. Arrives in 5-7 days
4. Review in-hand:
   - [ ] Colors accurate
   - [ ] Text readable
   - [ ] No print defects
   - [ ] Binding secure
   - [ ] Overall quality acceptable

**If issues found:**
- Update files on KDP (free to update)
- Order new proof
- Don't heavily market until satisfied with quality

### Step 3: Optimize Listing

**A+ Content (Brand Registry required)**
- Enhanced product descriptions with images
- Not available initially, but can apply later
- Requires brand registry (free but takes time)

**Request Additional Categories**
1. Email KDP support: kdp-support@amazon.com
2. Subject: "Request Additional Categories for [Book Title]"
3. Body:
```
Hello,

I would like to request additional categories for my book:

Title: [Your Book Title]
ASIN (paperback): [B0XXXXXXXXX]
ASIN (Kindle): [B0XXXXXXXXX]

Please add to the following BISAC categories:
- JUVENILE FICTION / Social Themes / Emotions & Feelings
- JUVENILE FICTION / Social Themes / Self-Esteem & Self-Reliance
- [Add up to 8 more specific categories]

Thank you!
```

**Update Keywords Based on Data**
- After 2-4 weeks, check which keywords drive sales
- Amazon Brand Registry allows backend keyword access
- Refine based on performance

## Part 5: Pricing and Promotion Strategy

### Initial Pricing Strategy

**Week 1-2: Launch Pricing**
- Price competitively to gain initial reviews
- Example: $9.99 print, $3.99 Kindle (or even $0.99 for 3 days)
- Goal: Get 5-10 reviews quickly

**Week 3-4: Raise to Target Price**
- Adjust to long-term pricing
- Example: $11.99 print, $4.99 Kindle
- Reviews make higher price more acceptable

### Promotional Tools (After Published)

**KDP Select Promotional Options:**

**1. Free Book Promotion (5 days per 90-day period)**
- Make Kindle version free for up to 5 days
- Great for launch or boosting visibility
- Drives downloads, reviews, and KU page reads after promo

**2. Kindle Countdown Deal (7 days per 90-day period)**
- Discount pricing for limited time
- Shows "countdown" timer on Amazon
- Maintains 70% royalty even at discounted price
- Example: $4.99 → $0.99 for 3 days

**When to use:**
- New book launch (free promo to get reviews)
- Slow sales month (countdown deal to boost)
- When launching book 2 in series (make book 1 free)

## Part 6: Book Review Strategy

Amazon reviews are CRUCIAL for sales. Here's how to get them ethically:

### Getting Your First 10 Reviews

**1. Friends and Family (2-3 reviews)**
- Ask people who actually read it to their kids
- Honest reviews only
- No "Review my book, I'll review yours" schemes

**2. Book Review Bloggers (3-5 reviews)**
- Search "children's book review blogs"
- Email with: free PDF copy, book description, ask if interested
- Be patient, many get hundreds of requests

**3. NetGalley or BookSirens (3-5 reviews)**
- Submit book for reviewer access
- Costs $0-50 depending on plan
- Legitimate review-generation platform

**4. Social Media Book Communities (2-4 reviews)**
- Join parent groups on Facebook
- Offer free copies in exchange for honest reviews
- Be transparent: "I'm offering free review copies"

**5. GiveawayTools or BookFunnel (varies)**
- Run a giveaway requiring Amazon review
- Legal if reviews are honest and disclosed

**Important:**
- NEVER buy fake reviews
- NEVER offer incentives for positive reviews
- NEVER review your own book or have competitors reviewed negatively
- Amazon's algorithms detect and punish this

### Review Request Email Template

```
Subject: Children's Book Review Opportunity - [Book Title]

Hi [Blogger Name],

I came across your blog [Blog Name] and loved your review of [Book They Reviewed]. I'm an author of children's books and wondered if you'd be interested in reviewing my new book, [Your Title].

[One-paragraph pitch about your book and why their audience would love it]

If you're interested, I'm happy to send:
- A free PDF review copy
- Or a print copy (I'll cover shipping)

No pressure if your review schedule is full—I understand! Either way, I appreciate the work you do to support children's literature.

Best,
[Your Name]
[Author Website]
```

## Troubleshooting Common Issues

### "My book isn't showing up in search"
- **Wait 24-72 hours** after "Live" status
- Check that categories and keywords are specific
- Search by exact title first, then by keywords
- Can take 1-2 weeks to index fully

### "Paperback and Kindle aren't linked"
- Email KDP support with both ASINs
- Usually linked within 24 hours

### "My cover looks pixelated on Amazon"
- Re-upload higher resolution cover image
- Kindle: minimum 2560 pixels on longest side
- Clear browser cache and check again

### "Print colors look wrong in proof"
- **Common issue:** Colors always slightly duller in print vs. screen
- Adjust files to be slightly more vibrant/saturated
- Avoid very dark backgrounds (prints even darker)

### "I need to make changes after publishing"
- You can update content/cover anytime
- Go to Bookshelf > "..." menu > "Edit paperback/ebook content"
- Changes go live in 24-72 hours
- ASIN stays the same, reviews remain
