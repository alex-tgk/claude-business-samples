# Sample Story: "Milo's Brave Day"

**Example children's book outline demonstrating the concepts from our prompts and templates.**

---

## Book Information

**Title:** Milo's Brave Day
**Subtitle:** A Story About Courage for Little Ones
**Age Range:** 3-5 years
**Theme:** Overcoming fear, building confidence
**Page Count:** 24 pages
**Format:** 8.5" x 8.5" picture book

---

## Story Summary

Milo the Mouse is excited about his first day at Sunny Forest School, but as the day approaches, he starts feeling scared. With help from his mom and new friends, Milo learns that being brave doesn't mean you're never scared—it means doing things even when you feel scared.

---

## Character Descriptions (For AI Generation)

**Main Character:**
- **Name:** Milo
- **Species:** Mouse
- **Age:** 4-5 years (child equivalent)
- **Appearance:** Small gray mouse with large round ears, bright brown eyes, wearing a blue striped shirt and tiny red backpack
- **Personality:** Curious, gentle, sometimes shy, caring
- **Visual Style:** Soft, friendly, Pixar-style character design with simple shapes

**Supporting Characters:**
- **Mom Mouse:** Loving, patient, wears a yellow dress
- **Luna (Bunny):** New friend at school, energetic, wears purple overalls
- **Teacher Owl:** Kind, wise, wears reading glasses
