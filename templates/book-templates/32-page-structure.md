# 32-Page Picture Book Structure Template

Standard template for a 32-page children's picture book (most common format).

---

## Page Breakdown

**Total Pages:** 32 (physical pages in book)
**Story Pages:** 24-28 pages
**Front/Back Matter:** 4-8 pages

---

## Detailed Page-by-Page Structure

### COVER (Outside)
**Front Cover:**
- Title
- Subtitle (if applicable)
- Author name
- Age range (optional)
- Main illustration (eye-catching, featuring protagonist)

**Spine:**
- Title
- Author name
- Publisher (optional)
- *Note: Only if 130+ pages (not typical for 32-page book)*

**Back Cover:**
- Book description (2-4 sentences)
- Author bio (1-2 sentences)
- Reviews/endorsements (if available)
- Barcode space (2" x 1.25" lower right)
- Age range
- Category/theme indicators
- Supporting illustration or color background

---

### INTERIOR PAGES

**Page 1 (Right side after opening cover):**
- Title page OR
- Half-title (title only) OR
- First page of story (modern approach)

**Page 2 (Left side, facing page 1):**
- Copyright page OR
- Blank OR
- Dedication OR
- Part of first illustration spread

**Copyright Page (Usually page 2 or 4):**
```
Copyright © [YEAR] by [YOUR NAME]
All rights reserved.

No part of this book may be reproduced in any form without permission from the publisher.

ISBN: [IF YOU HAVE ONE]

Published by [PUBLISHER NAME]
[CITY, STATE/COUNTRY]

Illustrations created with AI tools.
```

**Page 3-4 (Spread):**
- Story begins OR
- Full-spread illustration with story start

---

## Story Pages (Pages 5-28): MAIN CONTENT

### Act 1: Setup (Pages 5-10)
Introduce character, world, and problem/theme.

**Page 5-6 (Spread 1):**
- Introduce main character
- Show normal world
- Text: 15-25 words per page

**Page 7-8 (Spread 2):**
- Develop character personality
- Hint at theme or challenge

**Page 9-10 (Spread 3):**
- Inciting incident
- Problem or adventure begins

### Act 2: Rising Action (Pages 11-22)
Character faces challenges, learns, grows.

**Page 11-12 (Spread 4):**
- Character reacts to problem
- First attempt or exploration

**Page 13-14 (Spread 5):**
- Challenge increases or develops

**Page 15-16 (Spread 6):**
- Character tries different approach
- May fail (teaching resilience)

**Page 17-18 (Spread 7):**
- Deepening of theme
- Emotional moment

**Page 19-20 (Spread 8):**
- Building toward climax
- Character gaining understanding

**Page 21-22 (Spread 9):**
- Preparation for resolution
- Key realization or decision

### Act 3: Climax & Resolution (Pages 23-28)

**Page 23-24 (Spread 10):**
- Climax: character faces biggest challenge
- Uses what they've learned

**Page 25-26 (Spread 11):**
- Problem solved or lesson learned
- Emotional payoff

**Page 27-28 (Spread 12):**
- Return to (improved) normalcy
- Satisfying conclusion
- Reinforcement of theme

---

### BACK MATTER (Pages 29-32)

**Page 29-30 (Spread after story ends):**
**Option A:**
- "The End"
- Closing illustration

**Option B:**
- Author bio with photo
- Message to readers/parents

**Option C:**
- Activity page (discussion questions, coloring page)

**Page 31:**
**Option A:**
- "Also by [Author Name]" (catalog of your other books)

**Option B:**
- Author's note about the book's theme

**Option C:**
- Acknowledgments

**Page 32 (Inside back cover):**
**Option A:**
- Completely blank

**Option B:**
- More book recommendations

**Option C:**
- QR code to author website or free resources

---

## Alternative Structures

### 24-Page Book Structure
- Pages 1-2: Title, copyright
- Pages 3-20: Story (18 pages)
- Pages 21-22: The End
- Pages 23-24: Back matter

### 28-Page Book Structure
- Pages 1-2: Title, copyright
- Pages 3-24: Story (22 pages)
- Pages 25-26: The End
- Pages 27-28: Back matter

---

## Text Guidelines by Age

### Ages 2-4 (Board Book Style)
- 5-15 words per page
- Very simple sentences
- Repetitive phrases
- Focus on concepts (colors, numbers, animals)

### Ages 3-5 (Picture Book)
- 10-20 words per page
- Simple, clear sentences
- Some repetition for engagement
- Rhyming optional but popular

### Ages 5-7 (Early Reader Picture Book)
- 20-30 words per page
- Compound sentences okay
- More complex vocabulary
- Longer narratives

---

## Illustration Notes

**Full-Bleed vs. Bordered:**
- **Full-bleed:** Image extends to edge of page (requires 0.125" bleed)
- **Bordered:** Image has white space around it (easier to print)

**Text Placement:**
- Keep text in "safe zone" (0.5" from all edges)
- Ensure high contrast between text and background
- Consider text boxes or overlays if needed

**Spreads (Two-Page Illustrations):**
- Account for gutter (center binding area)
- Important elements should not be in the center 1" where binding occurs
- Test how spread looks when book is open

## Checklist for 32-Page Book

### Structure
- [ ] Front cover designed
- [ ] Back cover designed
- [ ] Title page (page 1 or 3)
- [ ] Copyright page (page 2 or 4)
- [ ] Story pages: 20-28 pages
- [ ] Ending page ("The End")
- [ ] Back matter (author info, other books, etc.)
- [ ] Total page count: 32 pages (even number required)

### Content
- [ ] Story has clear beginning, middle, end
- [ ] Each page advances the story
- [ ] Illustrations match text
- [ ] Text is age-appropriate
- [ ] Reading level verified
- [ ] No typos or errors

### Technical
- [ ] All images 300 DPI or higher
- [ ] Bleed set correctly (0.125" if full-bleed)
- [ ] Text in safe zone (not too close to edges)
- [ ] Page numbers correct (if used)
- [ ] Spreads align correctly
- [ ] Copyright information included

## Example Page Flows

### Emotional/Educational Book (e.g., "Learning About Anger")

**Act 1 (Pages 5-10):**
- Pages 5-6: Meet happy character in their normal day
- Pages 7-8: Something frustrating happens
- Pages 9-10: Character feels angry for the first time

**Act 2 (Pages 11-22):**
- Pages 11-12: Character expresses anger poorly (yelling, hitting)
- Pages 13-14: Consequences of poor anger expression
- Pages 15-16: Character feels bad, doesn't know what to do
- Pages 17-18: A wise friend/parent teaches about feelings
- Pages 19-20: Character learns about breathing, talking, taking breaks
- Pages 21-22: Character practices new skills

**Act 3 (Pages 23-28):**
- Pages 23-24: Character gets frustrated again (test moment)
- Pages 25-26: Character uses new skills successfully!
- Pages 27-28: Character and friends are happy, lesson reinforced

### Adventure Book (e.g., "The Great Treasure Hunt")

**Act 1 (Pages 5-10):**
- Pages 5-6: Character finds mysterious map
- Pages 7-8: Decides to go on adventure
- Pages 9-10: Journey begins

**Act 2 (Pages 11-22):**
- Pages 11-12: First location, first clue
- Pages 13-14: Challenge #1
- Pages 15-16: Second location, second clue
- Pages 17-18: Challenge #2, meets helper
- Pages 19-20: Third location, final clue
- Pages 21-22: Gets close to treasure

**Act 3 (Pages 23-28):**
- Pages 23-24: Finds treasure!
- Pages 25-26: Discovers treasure is [friendship/knowledge/etc.]
- Pages 27-28: Returns home changed, happy ending

---

## Notes

- Most children's picture books are 32 pages (industry standard)
- Page count must be divisible by 4 for printing (8, 16, 24, 32, 40, etc.)
- "Page 1" traditionally starts on the right side when you open the book
- Left pages are even numbers, right pages are odd numbers
- Spreads should be designed as connected illustrations

---

**Use this structure as a starting point and adapt based on your specific story needs.**
